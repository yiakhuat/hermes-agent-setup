Our values are a commitment to our customers and a signal of the culture we're building.

Simplicity

01

Our technology is complex. Our customers' problems are complex. Our solutions can't be. Complexity appreciates, and simplicity scales — so we obsess over first principles and apply them to every decision, big and small.

Decisiveness

02

AI moves fast. There's no time for overanalysis or ivory tower thinking. We have a bias for action and we'd rather make mistakes than become complacent. We make the call, course-correct, and learn as we go.

Job’s Not Finished

03

There is always more we can do to solve for our customers, and we know it. Harvey is an intense culture — we hire people who thrive on that intensity, who push each other for excellence. We're never satisfied with the status quo.
